Navic Guide Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Navic Guide is a navigation app by Harish Deepak. It is not affiliated with ISRO or the Government of India.

What the app collects

You can use the app's navigation features without an account. The app has no analytics and the developer runs no server beyond the account system described below.

An account is optional. Signing up requires an email address, a password, and a username. The developer does not see your password: it is handled directly by Google Firebase, the service the app uses for accounts and friend data (see "Accounts and friends" and "Data sent to third-party services" below).

Location

The app reads your precise location on your device. It uses it to show your position, guide you along a route, and detect when you leave the route. On Android, the app also reads satellite status from your phone (which satellites, including NavIC, are visible and used). This location and satellite data stay on your device and are not sent anywhere, unless you turn on location sharing as described below.

Accounts and friends

If you create an account, you choose a username. Other users see this username, not your email address. You can change your username at any time.

To see another user's location on the map, you send them a friend request using their username. They must accept it before any connection is made. Either of you can remove the friend link at any time, which also stops any location sharing between you.

Sharing your live location with accepted friends is a separate setting, off by default. You turn it on yourself in the app, and can turn it off at any time. While it is on, your device sends your current coordinates to Firebase so your accepted friends who are using the app can see your position on their map. Friends who are not accepted, and anyone without the app, cannot see your location.

You can delete your account from inside the app. This removes your username, your friend links, and your stored account data, including any location last shared.

Data sent to third-party services

The app sends the following to public services to work. Their own policies apply.

  • Map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Your device requests tiles for the area you view.
  • Place search from Nominatim (OpenStreetMap). The text you type in the search box is sent.
  • Routing from OSRM, routing.openstreetmap.de, and openrailrouting.org. The start and end coordinates of your route are sent.
  • Account sign-in, your username, and friend and location-sharing data are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), only if you create an account. Google's privacy policy applies to this data: policies.google.com/privacy.

These services receive your device's IP address as part of any web request. If you use "My location" as the start, your current coordinates go to the routing service.

Voice guidance

Spoken directions use your device's text-to-speech engine. The app does not record audio.

Sharing and selling

The developer does not sell your personal data. Your location is shared only with friends you have explicitly added and only while you have location sharing turned on, as described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
