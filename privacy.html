Navic Guide Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Navic Guide is a navigation app by Harish Deepak. It is not affiliated with ISRO or the Government of India.

What the app collects

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. The developer runs no server and receives no data from the app.

Location

The app reads your precise location on your device. It uses it to show your position, guide you along a route, and detect when you leave the route. On Android, the app also reads satellite status from your phone (which satellites, including NavIC, are visible and used). Location and satellite data stay on your device. The app does not store them and does not send them to the developer.

Data sent to third-party services

The app sends the following to public services to work. Their own policies apply.

  • Map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Your device requests tiles for the area you view.
  • Place search from Nominatim (OpenStreetMap). The text you type in the search box is sent.
  • Routing from OSRM, routing.openstreetmap.de, and openrailrouting.org. The start and end coordinates of your route are sent.

These services receive your device's IP address as part of any web request. If you use "My location" as the start, your current coordinates go to the routing service.

Voice guidance

Spoken directions use your device's text-to-speech engine. The app does not record audio.

Sharing and selling

The developer does not collect, share, or sell your personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
